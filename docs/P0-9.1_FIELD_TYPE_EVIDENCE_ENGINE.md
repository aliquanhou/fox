## 2. 实现方法

### 2.1 证据标签系统

每个字段收集多个证据标签：
- `high-freq` (>=10 accesses)
- `med-freq` (3-9 accesses)
- `struct-member` (in consecutive cluster)

### 2.2 类型候选规则

| 条件 | 候选类型 | 置信度 |
|------|----------|--------|
| in cluster AND >=5 accesses | `struct-field` | MEDIUM |
| >=10 accesses | `DWORD/state` | MEDIUM |
| 3-9 accesses | `DWORD` | LOW |
| 1-2 accesses | `UNKNOWN` | insufficient evidence |

### 2.3 输出格式

```c
/* P0-8.5 Structure Evidence:
 * Object: global_46E920
 *   Fields: 3 unique, 3 total accesses
 *   +0xDBC844   size:4  accesses:1    candidate:UNKNOWN (insufficient evidence)
 *     evidence: struct-member
 *   +0xDBC848   size:4  accesses:1    candidate:UNKNOWN (insufficient evidence)
 *     evidence: struct-member
 *   +0xDBC84C   size:4  accesses:1    candidate:UNKNOWN (insufficient evidence)
 *     evidence: struct-member
 *   Clusters (1): +0xDBC844..+0xDBC84C (12 bytes, 3 fields)
 *   Confidence: MEDIUM
 */
```
