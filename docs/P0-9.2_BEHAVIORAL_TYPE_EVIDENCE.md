## 2. 实现方法

### 2.1 行为类型推断规则

| 条件 | 候选类型 | 置信度 |
|------|----------|--------|
| in cluster AND >=5 accesses | `struct-field` | MEDIUM |
| >=20 accesses | `BooleanLike (high-frequency state)` | MEDIUM |
| >=10 accesses | `DWORD/state` | LOW-MEDIUM |
| >=3 accesses | `DWORD` | LOW |
| 1-2 accesses | `UNKNOWN` | insufficient |

### 2.2 输出格式

```c
/* P0-8.5 Structure Evidence:
 * Object: global_46E920
 *   Fields: 9 unique, 24 total accesses
 *   +0x14       size:4  accesses:1    candidate:UNKNOWN (insufficient confidence)
 *     evidence: struct-member
 *   +0xDBDD44   size:4  accesses:12   candidate:DWORD/state (LOW-MEDIUM confidence)
 *     evidence: high-freq
 *   Clusters (1): +0x14..+0x18 (8 bytes, 2 fields)
 *   Confidence: MEDIUM
 */
```
